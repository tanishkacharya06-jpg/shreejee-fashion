v17.0
- Forgot password / reset: "Forgot password?" on the login page. The customer enters their email and the phone number on their account; if both match, the request appears in Admin > Password resets (with a badge). The owner presses "Generate code" and sends the one-time 6-digit code to the customer's phone on WhatsApp (one-tap button; the code is shown only once). The customer enters email + code + new password at /reset-password. Codes work for 30 minutes, once, and lock after 5 wrong tries. Customers get a "Password changed" notification. Admin accounts and the shared default customer account cannot use this page. No email service is needed.
- WhatsApp button: the floating WhatsApp button is removed from the home page and every other page. On a product page it appears only after the customer taps/clicks the product photo (tap again to hide). The WhatsApp links in the footer, About page, order help and return help are unchanged.
- Three website looks, chosen by the customer: Classic (existing deep-green look), Modern (indigo & coral, centred banner, Home/Shop/Saved/Cart/Account buttons in a bottom bar, menu button on the right) and Festive Boutique (maroon & gold, banner on the left, stacked header with menu links under the logo, uppercase buttons, cart bar bottom-left). Each look changes colours, shapes, banner layout, hero wording, quote and button placement. A picker opens on a new visitor's first visit; afterwards use "Look" in the header, "Change website look" in the menu, or My account. The choice is saved on the customer's account (follows them to another phone) and in a small cookie (sf_ui) for guests. Admins always see Classic.
- Hero text edited in Website Editor applies to the Classic look. Modern and Festive use their own banner words and quote.
- Cookies page mentions the sf_ui cookie.
