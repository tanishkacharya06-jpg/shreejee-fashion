Privacy policy.

A plain-language overview for customers.

We use the information you provide to create an account, prepare and deliver orders, answer questions and send store updates. Information may include your name, email, phone number, delivery address, order items and payment status.

How information is used

  • To manage your account, basket and order history.
  • To tell you about a new product or an announcement posted by the store.
  • To help the store administrator review QR orders, arrange delivery and export order reports.
  • To protect accounts and investigate misuse.

Payments

Cash on delivery is handled by the store. If an online payment provider is configured, payment processing should take place through that provider. This website is not designed to collect card numbers, UPI PINs or banking passwords directly.

Storage and sharing

Order and account information is stored in the store's application database. It is available to authorised store administrators for store operations. Do not deploy this demo on a public server until you have configured HTTPS, secure secrets, database backups, access controls and a real privacy contact process.

Your choices

Contact the store owner to ask about correcting account details or requesting account closure. Order records may need to be retained for legitimate operational or legal purposes.

{% elif page=='terms' %}

Terms & conditions.

Please review before ordering.

Product listings and prices

Product images, descriptions, sizes, availability and prices are listed for customer convenience. Availability and final delivery details should be confirmed by the store before fulfilment.

Orders

A QR cart is a secure order reference for the store to review; it is not by itself proof of payment. Cash-on-delivery orders are sent to the store for confirmation. An online order is treated as paid only after the configured payment provider verifies the payment.

Delivery and cancellations

Delivery timing, delivery charges, exchanges, returns and cancellations should be confirmed directly with Shreejee Fashion before the order is fulfilled. The owner should add the actual store-specific return and delivery policy before public launch.

Account access

Please keep your password private. The store may restrict an account if it is being misused. A verified customer status is managed by a store administrator.

Cookies & tracking.

What this website uses.

Essential session storage

This website uses a Flask session cookie to remember login status and the current shopping cart. It is needed for normal operation.

Look preference

If you choose a website look (Classic, Modern App, Festive Boutique, Midnight Luxe, Fresh Pop or Editorial), a small cookie called sf_ui remembers your choice for up to a year. It stores only the name of the look. Menu placement follows the selected visual theme automatically.

Analytics and advertising

No third-party analytics or advertising tracking has been included by default. If you later add analytics, marketing pixels or embedded services, update this page and provide any consent mechanism required by the laws that apply to your customers.

Browser controls

You can block or clear cookies through your browser settings. Blocking session cookies may prevent login, cart and checkout functions from working.

Deployment note

Use HTTPS in production and set a strong secret key. This demo does not currently set a custom cookie-consent banner because it does not include non-essential tracking by default.
